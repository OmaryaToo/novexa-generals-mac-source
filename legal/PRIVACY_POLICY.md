## 1. What this covers
The NOVEXA Generals Mac launcher, the NOVEXA login page (`play.novexa-jo.com/login`) and the NOVEXA Generals Online
game service (`play.novexa-jo.com`). NOVEXA is not affiliated with Electronic Arts, Valve (Steam) or Discord.

## 2. The launcher (on your Mac)
The launcher sends **no telemetry** and has no analytics. Settings, logs and backups stay on your Mac in
`~/Library/Application Support/NOVEXA Generals Mac`. If you use **Download from Steam**, you sign in to Valve's own
SteamCMD tool in Terminal. Your Steam password and Steam Guard code go directly to Valve, and NOVEXA never receives them.

## 3. Data we process when you play online
| Data | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Discord user ID and username | To create and recognise your account (we request only the `identify` scope, **not your email**) | Discord, when you sign in |
| In-game display name | Shown to other players | Derived from your Discord name |
| IP address (last used) | Security, abuse prevention and connecting players for matches | Your connection |
| Device / client identifiers | Security, ban enforcement and anti-cheat checks | The game client |
| Game-file checksums (CRCs) | Making sure everyone plays the same game version (fair play) | The game client |
| Match history, results, ratings (ELO), statistics | Leaderboards, matchmaking, match history | Your games |
| Friends and blocked-player lists, lobby presence | Social features | Your actions in the game |
| Session tokens | Keeping you signed in | Our server |
| Server logs | Operating and securing the service | Our server |

We **do not** collect your email, real name, payment details or Discord messages, and we **do not** sell
your data or use it for advertising.

## 4. Who else processes data
* **Discord**: sign-in (Discord's own privacy policy applies).
* **Cloudflare**: network protection and HTTPS in front of our server (they process IP addresses).
* **Other players** see your display name, presence, lobby details and match results. During a match, game traffic
  may flow directly between players' computers (peer-to-peer), so other players may be able to see your IP address.

## 5. Retention
Account, statistics and match data are kept while your account exists. Server logs are kept for [30] days.

## 6. Your rights
You can ask us to **access, correct or delete** your data, or delete your account, by contacting
**[privacy contact email]** from the Discord account concerned. You can also revoke NOVEXA's access in
Discord → User Settings → Authorized Apps. If GDPR applies to you, you may also complain to your local
data-protection authority.

## 7. Children
The service is not directed at children under [13 / 16 depending on your country]. If you believe a child has created an
account, contact us and we will delete it.

We will post changes on this page and update the date above.
